How your pool gets built

Most builds take about 9 to 10 weeks from design to first swim, depending on permits and weather. You'll know what's next at every stage.

Start your design
  1. 1Weeks 1 to 2

    Design and 3D rendering

    We walk your yard, talk budget, and show you the pool in 3D before anything is signed.

  2. 2Weeks 2 to 4

    Permits and approvals

    We handle city permits and HOA submittals and let you know when they clear.

  3. 3Week 5

    Dig and steel

    Layout, excavation and the rebar cage that gives the shell its strength.

  4. 4Week 6

    Plumbing and gunite

    Lines, equipment pad and electrical, then the concrete shell is shot.

  5. 5Weeks 7 to 8

    Tile, coping and decking

    Waterline tile, stone or travertine coping, and the deck around it.

  6. 6Week 9

    Plaster and startup

    Interior finish, fill and startup, plus a walkthrough so you know your equipment.

    What happens at every visit

    Same tech, same day each week. Gulf Coast heat and pollen move fast, so we check everything, every time.

    Water

    • Test and balance chlorine, pH and alkalinity
    • Check salt level and cell on saltwater pools
    • All chemicals included

    Cleaning

    • Skim the surface and empty skimmer and pump baskets
    • Brush walls, steps and the tile line
    • Vacuum the floor and spa

    Equipment

    • Check pump, filter pressure, heater and automation
    • Flag leaks and worn parts before they fail
    • Repairs handled by the crew that installs the gear

    A report after every visit

    You get a text with a photo of your pool and the day's water readings, so you always know it was serviced and what we found.

    Questions homeowners ask

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Most customers are at work during their visit. We just need gate access and a heads-up about pets.

    Are chemicals included?

    Yes, on every plan. Big fixes like a green-to-clean recovery or a full drain are quoted before we start.

    Do you service pools you didn't build?

    Yes. We service any residential pool in our area, whoever built it.

    What happens after a storm?

    Weekly customers get first call on storm cleanups. We clear debris, rebalance the water and check equipment for damage.

    Is there a contract?

    No. Service is month to month, and you can pause or cancel before your next billing date.
